This extension is a derivative of NSA Ghidra's 68000 processor language and
Brad Ackerman/backerman's CPU32 patch:
https://github.com/NationalSecurityAgency/ghidra/commit/fafd1bb00aaca30ee546de0485896ba4de1bacab

Local modifications: isolated CPU32_STATUS_V2 language identifier, T0 flag,
SR/CCR packing/unpacking and RTR data-effect fixes. They do not constitute
complete CPU32 hardware, exception, peripheral or TBL semantics.
